Explain the most important reason a reviewer should be cautious.

## GenLayer Fit Pressure Test

Challenge whether GenLayer is actually needed or whether the product would work similarly with a centralized backend, simple oracle, normal contract, or local simulation. Pressure-test whether the use case justifies GenLayer's cost, latency, and validator-consensus overhead, or whether it mainly needs a faster off-chain AI/backend workflow. For advisory or recommendation products, call out stale-knowledge and unverifiable-evidence risks when the contract does not check current facts through web-fetching or another verifiable source.

## Contract / Tooling Pressure Test

Challenge whether the contract/tooling really supports the product claim. Call out weak EP/validator logic, shape-only validation, cosmetic non-determinism, wrong method mapping, source/deploy drift, or missing contract-side verification. For LLM-heavy projects, check whether validators test domain-specific facts and meaningful results, or mostly approve plausibility, coherence, safety, formatting, or generic usefulness.

## Engineering Pressure Test

Challenge implementation quality: scaffolded code, fragile integration, missing setup path, unclear architecture, app simulation, or weak error handling.
