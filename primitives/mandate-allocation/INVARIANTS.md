# Mandate Allocation - Invariants

- Output is evaluative/recommendatory and creates no executable authority.
- Every result binds the exact mandate and evaluated opportunity/evidence set.
- `UNDETERMINED` cannot be interpreted as approved.
- Recommended amounts cannot bypass deterministic hard constraints.
- A recommendation cannot reserve capital, encumber a claim, assign a replay lane, or move value.
- DAA must independently create any downstream authority grant.
