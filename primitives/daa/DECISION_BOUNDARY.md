# DAA — Decision Boundary

`JUDGMENT_BOUNDARY = NONE` for the v0.1 canonical slice. Qualitative mandate interpretation is externalized to Policy Envelope / Claim Verification before requesting; DAA v0.1 allocates authority through deterministic predicates only. The historical judgment examples below remain valid future extensions requiring a new capability version.

Examples:
- interpreting a qualitative authority-allocation mandate;
- resolving which already-admissible actor should receive a bounded authority grant;
- deriving bounded conditions attached to that authority.

GenLayer must not turn DAA into a general underwriting or opportunity-ranking engine.

The following remain deterministic:
- authority-source identity;
- request and maximum bounds;
- resource/capital capacity supplied to DAA;
- award uniqueness/versioning;
- validity and expiry;
- claim identity binding where used;
- downstream encumbrance;
- capital commitment;
- replay/lane assignment;
- settlement.
