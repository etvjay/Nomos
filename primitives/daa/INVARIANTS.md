# DAA - Invariants

- DAA output is a bounded authority grant, not merely a recommendation.
- Every award identifies authority source, beneficiary, governed resource/capital scope, purpose, bounds, policy and validity.
- Rejected and undetermined outcomes create no authority.
- Finalized awards are immutable/versioned.
- Award bounds cannot exceed deterministic limits supplied by the authority source or current resource state.
- DAA does not reserve capital.
- DAA does not encumber claims.
- DAA does not assign replay/nonces/lanes.
- DAA does not itself move value.
- Downstream actions must prove they are within the award.
- DAA must not absorb generic underwriting, broad opportunity ranking, or evidence verification merely because an intelligent substrate can perform them.
